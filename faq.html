Over half of our menu is dedicated to vegetarian cuisine. Our creations, including the Truffle Butter Paneer Tikka, Wild Morel Mushroom Risotto, and 36-hour charcoal Dal Makhani, are crafted with the exact same rigor and complexity as our meat dishes.
Yes. We have multiple 100% plant-based items clearly marked on our menu, such as our Pulled Jackfruit Tacos in jowar millet shells, Crispy Lotus Stem Honey Chilli, and Coconut Tempered Baingan. Additionally, our chefs are happy to adapt select vegetarian dishes to be completely dairy-free.
Yes, takeaway is available during all operational dining hours. We pack all takeout orders in custom thermal biodegradable boxes that preserve plating aesthetics, aromatic steam, and texture during transport.

Yes, our main dining room easily seats groups of 8 to 14 guests together. For parties of 8 or more, we recommend our curated Chef’s Tasting Family Menu to ensure synchronized course delivery and an effortless dining flow.
