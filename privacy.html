Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Urban Eats

Showcase Notice: Urban Eats is a fictional modern Indian restaurant concept created as a high-end portfolio demonstration by MNW Creative Studio. This Privacy Policy outlines industry-standard privacy practices applicable to contemporary hospitality web platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Urban Eats website, make a table reservation, or send an inquiry, we collect information that enables us to provide exceptional dining service. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, phone number, and email address.
  • Dining Preferences: Seating preferences, party size, target dining dates and times, occasion markers (anniversaries, birthdays), and special notes.
  • Health & Allergy Disclosures: Dietary restrictions, food allergies, and preferences you voluntarily submit.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device characteristics, IP address, and interaction patterns gathered through cookie technologies.

2. How We Use Information

Urban Eats uses the information collected solely for legitimate hospitality purposes:

  • Processing, verifying, and managing your dining table reservations and private suite bookings.
  • Sending immediate booking receipts and updates via email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • Communicating allergen notifications to our kitchen brigade and service staff prior to your arrival.
  • Improving our website performance, layout responsiveness, and user experience.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use functional session cookies and local storage tokens to remember your dietary filters, temporary reservation session drafts, and cookie consent preferences. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Services

We may collaborate with vetted third-party service providers to power specific website features:

  • Maps & Geolocation: To provide driving directions and route planning to our LOCATION restaurant.
  • Messaging Platforms: Direct WhatsApp communication APIs to facilitate conversational concierge assistance.
  • Cloud Hosting: Secure cloud infrastructure adhering to industry encryption benchmarks.

5. Data Retention

Reservation records and contact inquiries are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill table management obligations, address guest feedback, or comply with relevant accounting regulations. Guests may request removal of their contact details at any time.

6. Security Measures

We implement technical, organizational, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All form submissions transmit over SSL/TLS encrypted connections.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable data protection frameworks, you maintain the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact records.
  • Request erasure of your reservation history and contact information.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications or optional cookies at any time.

8. Updates to This Policy

Urban Eats may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational procedures or applicable privacy laws. The updated version will always be indicated by the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document.
