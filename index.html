Our Heritage

Born From The City.
Inspired By The Plate.

is a city founded on architectural boldness, clean geometry, and progressive thought. Urban Eats was conceived with that exact spirit.

“We asked ourselves: What happens when you honor the nostalgic warmth of Indian clay-oven cooking, but apply the finesse and minimalism of modern culinary design?”

The result is a culinary journey where smoke, velvet textures, and vibrant spices tell stories of both ancient markets and cosmopolitan dining rooms. Every dish is composed with geometric balance, purposeful simplicity, and unapologetic flavour.

The Culinary Mind

Chef Arjun Mehta

“Indian food has never been shy about flavor. Our role today is not to dilute that boldness, but to frame it with modern precision and elegance.”

Chef Arjun brings together classical Indian techniques and contemporary global influences. Having trained in esteemed kitchens across world culinary capitals, his cuisine is characterized by respect for traditional tandoor methods combined with modern sous-vide precision and creative textural pairings.

At Urban Eats, Chef Arjun personally curates each seasonal rotation, working directly with dedicated regional farmers to source heirloom ingredients that give each dish its distinct, memorable identity.
