Cookies & Transparency

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Urban Eats

Overview: This Cookie Policy explains how Urban Eats uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to enhance your experience when navigating our modern Indian dining showcase.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences between pages, and provide aggregated anonymous analytical insights.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

A. Strictly Essential Cookies

These cookies are mandatory for the website to function properly. They facilitate table reservation workflows, maintain active booking modal states, secure form submissions, and record your privacy preferences. These cookies cannot be deactivated.

B. Preference & Functional Storage

These tokens remember your personalized settings—such as whether you filtered the menu by Vegetarian or Vegan options, your preferred dining party size, and audio-visual playback settings.

C. Performance & Analytics Cookies

We collect anonymized telemetry regarding page load speeds, high-traffic menu categories, and device screen dimensions to optimize the site layout across mobile, tablet, and desktop environments.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can modify your cookie choices at any time directly through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies or cookies from specific websites.
  • Configure your browser to block all cookies automatically upon closing your session.
